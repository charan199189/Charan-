Scam Awareness

Recognize social engineering tactics and stay ahead of attackers.

🔐

1. OTP & Credential Scams

Golden Rule: Never share One-Time Passwords (OTP) or PINs with anyone. Real banks or delivery agents will never call or message to solicit your verification codes.

🔗

2. Fake & Deceptive Links

Check the exact domain address before clicking. Attackers spoof names using numbers (e.g. paypa1.com) or cheap abuse TLDs like .xyz, .top, or .buzz.

📷

3. QR Code "Quishing"

QR codes can easily redirect you to fraudulent login portals or trigger malicious dialers. Never scan unverified stickers pasted over public parking meters or menus.

⏱️

4. Urgency & Coercion Scams

Messages warning that your account is "suspended immediately" or threatening legal arrest are engineered to provoke uncritical panic. Pause, breathe, and verify independently.

🌐

5. Fake Website Portals

Just because a page has an SSL padlock does not mean it is trustworthy. Anyone can register a free HTTPS certificate on a scam domain. Inspect the full domain name.

🎁

6. Unsolicited Prizes & Gifts

If you didn't enter a lottery, you didn't win one. Demands for "processing fees" or gift card purchases to release lottery winnings are always 100% scams.

Safety Alerts & Tips

⚠️ Trending: Postal Package Redelivery Scams

Scammers are blasting SMS messages claiming a parcel has an unpaid $1.50 customs fee. Do not tap the link.

🔒 Multi-Factor Authentication Alert

Never read 6-digit OTP codes aloud or text them back to anyone, even if they claim to be your bank representative.

📷 QR Code Parking Meter Warnings

Inspect parking meter payment stickers. Fraudulent stickers are often glued over genuine payment QR codes.
